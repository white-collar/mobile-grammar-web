Unit 10 - Will be doing and will have done

Unit 10

Will be doing and will have done

(a) Спершу розгляньте ситуацію:

 

Анна телефонує своїй подрузі. Вона хоче запросити її в кіно:

 

- Mary, what about going to the cinema at 12 o'clock?

- I'm sorry, but I can't. I promised mom to help her in the garden.

I'll be planting flowers this time tomorrow.

- Then what about going to the cinema at six?

- Well, at 6 o'clock I'll be cleaning my room, but I'll have cleaned it

in half an hour, so we can go to the cinema at 7 o'clock.

 

'll be planting, 'll be cleaning — форми Future Continuous Tense

 

 

Ці форми Future Continuous(will be doing),

схожі на Past(was doing) і Present Continuous (am/is/are doing), ми використовуємо, щоб сказати, що дія відбуватиметься в певний час у майбутньому.

У наступних прикладах порівняйте will be doing з іншими формами continuous:

 

- My friend has her English lessons every day at 5 o'clock.

- Yesterday at 5 o'clock she was having her English lesson. (див.Unit 12)

- It is 5 o'clock now she is having her English lesson. (див.Unit 1)

- At 5 o'clock tomorrow she will be having her English lesson.

 

(b) У першому прикладі 'll have cleaned є дієсловом у Future Perfect Tense. Ми використовуємо цей час (will have done), щоб сказати, що щось уже станеться до певного моменту в майбутньому.

Ось ще кілька прикладів:

 

- He says that before he leaves (this is a certain moment in the future),

he'll have seen every show in the town.

- By this time next month, all the roses will have died.

- When you are my age you'll have learnt much about the weakness of man.

 

3 Ви можете також використовувати Future Continuous Tense (will be doing) в інших випадках: щоб сказати про дії, які вже заплановані або вирішені:

 

- Here is John's schedule for the next Saturday:

-in the morning he will be working in the garden.

-in the afternoon, when it is very hot he will be repairing his

car in the garage.

- And what is he doing in the evening? He is taking Mary to the cinema.
