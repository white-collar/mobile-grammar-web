Unit 60 - Verb + preposition + -ing

Unit 60

Verb + preposition + -ing

 

(a) Багато дієслів входять у конструкцію

дієслово (V) + прийменник (P) + додаток.

Наприклад, talk about:

 

- We talked about the problem. (about – це прийменник)

 

Якщо додаток є дієсловом, то воно

закінчується на -ing:

 

- We talked about going to America. (V + P + -ing)

 

Ось кілька дієслів, які входять у

конструкцію V + P + -ing:

 

succeed in

Has Tom succeeded

in

finding a job yet?

feel like (*)

I don't feel

like

going out tonight.

think about/of

Are you thinking

of/about

buying a house?

dream of

I've always dreamed

of

being rich.

approve/disapprove of

She doesn't approve

of

gambling.

look forward to

I'm looking forward

to

meeting her.

insist on

He insisted

on

buying me a drink.

decide against

We decided

against

moving to London.

apologise for

He apologised

for

keeping me waiting.

 

(*) I feel like doing = Мені хотілося б зробити, я схильний

зробити.

 

Кажуть `apologise to someone for something':

- He apologised to me for keeping me waiting. (а не `he apologised me')

 

Деякі дієслова входять у конструкцію

дієслово + прийменник + хтось + -ing.

Наприклад:

 

- We are all looking forward to Peter coming home.

- She doesn't approve of her son staying out late at night.

- They insisted on me (або my) staying with them. (Див. Unit 35c.)

 

(b) Ці дієслова входять у конструкцію

дієслово + додаток + прийменник + -ing:

 

accuse

They accused

me

of

telling lies.

suspect

Did they suspect

the man

of

being a spy?

congratulate

I congratulated

Ann

on

passing the exam.

prevent

What prevented

him

from

coming to the wedding?

stop

We stopped

everyone

from (*)

leaving the building.

thank

I thanked

her

for

being so helpful.

forgive

Please forgive

me

for

not writing to you.

warn

They warned

us

against

buying the car.

 

(*) Після stop можна пропускати from. Отже, ви можете

сказати:

 

- We stopped everyone leaving (або from leaving) the building.

 

Деякі з цих дієслів часто використовуються в

пасивному стані:

 

- I was accused of telling lies.

- Was the man suspected of being a spy?

- We were warned against buying it.
