Unit 64 - Prefer and would rather

Unit 64

Prefer and would rather

 

(a) Prefer to do і prefer doing

Щоб сказати про перевагу, кажуть

`prefer to do' або `prefer doing'.

Вираз `Prefer to do' уживається частіше:

 

- I don't like cities. I prefer to live (або I prefer living) in the country.

 

Запам’ятайте різницю конструкцій:

 

I prefer (doing) something to (doing) something else

але: I prefer to do something rather than (do) something else

 

 

- I prefer that coat to the coat you were wearing yesterday.

- Tom prefers driving to travelling by train.

але: Tom prefers to drive rather than travel by train.

- I prefer to live in the country rather than (live) in a city.

 

(b) Would prefer (to do)

 

Щоб висловити перевагу в конкретній ситуації

(а не взагалі), використовують `would prefer to do':

 

- `Would you prefer tea or coffee?' `Coffee, please.'

- `Shall we go by train?' `Well, I'd prefer to go by car.' (а не `going')

 

Зверніть увагу на конструкцію:

 

I'd prefer to do something rather than (do) something else

 

- I'd prefer to stay at home tonight rather than go to the cinema.

 

(c) Would rather (do) = would prefer to do.

Інфінітив після would rather використовується

без to.

 

Порівняйте:

Shall we go by train?

Well, I'd prefer to go by car.

 

Well, I'd rather go by car. (а не `to go')

 

- `Would you rather have tea or coffee?' `Coffee, please.'

- I'm tired. I'd rather not go out this evening, if you don't mind.

- `Do you want to go out this evening?' `I'd rather not.'

 

Зверніть увагу на конструкцію:

 

I'd rather do something than (do) something else

 

- I'd rather stay at home than go to the cinema.

 

(d) Would rather someone did something

 

Коли хочуть, щоб хтось інший щось

зробив, кажуть I'd rather did../

I'd rather he did...тощо. У конструкції

використовується минулий час, але значення

цієї конструкції — теперішній або майбутній час,

але не минулий.

 

Порівняйте:

 

I'd rather cook the dinner now.

I'd rather you cooked the dinner now. (а не `I'd rather you cook')

 

- `Shall I stay here?' `Well, I'd rather you came with us.'

- I'd rather you didn't tell anyone what I said.

- `Do you mind if I smoke?' `I'd rather you didn't.'
