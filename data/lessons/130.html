Unit 130

Phrasal verbs (get up, break down, fill in etc.)

(a) Дієслова часто вживають із такими словами:

on off in out up down away back

over about round forward through along

Ми часто вживаємо такі слова з дієсловами

руху. Наприклад:

 

get on The bus was full. We couldn't get on.

drive off She got into the car and drove off.

come back Tom is leaving tomorrow and coming back on Saturday.

turn round When I touched him on the shoulder, he turned round.

 

Однак часто ці слова (on/off/up/down тощо)

змінюють зміст дієслова.

Наприклад:

 

- Sorry I'm late. The car broke down.

- Look out! There's a car coming.

- It was my first flight. I was very nervous as the plane took off.

- I was so tired this morning that I couldn't get up.

- How did you get on in your examination yesterday?

 

Такі словосполучення (break down/get up/get on..)

є фразовими дієсловами.

 

(b) Іноді фразові дієслова мають додаток.

Зазвичай можливі дві позиції цих додатків.

Отже, можна сказати:

 

додаток додаток

 

I turned off the light. або I turned the light off.

 

Ось ще кілька прикладів:

 

- Could you

fill in this form?

fill this form in?

- It's warm.

Take off your coat.

Take your coat off.

- The fire-brigade soon arrived and

put out the fire.

put the fire out.

- I think I'll

throw away these old newspapers.

throw these old newspapers away.

- The police got into the house by

breaking down the door.

breaking the door down.

 

Іноді додатком до фразового дієслова

є займенник (it/them/me/you/him/her/us).

Такі займенники стоять перед on off in out

up down тощо:

 

- They gave me a form and told me to fill it in. (але не `fill in it')

- Ann's asleep. Don't wake her up. (але не `wake up her')

- `What shall I do with these old newspapers?' `Throw them away.'

- Here's the money you need. Don't forget to pay me back.

 

(c) Іноді вживають конструкцію

фразове дієслово + прийменник.

Наприклад:look forward to / keep up with/

cut down on.

Додаток завжди ставиться після прийменника:

 

- Are you looking forward to your holiday?

- You're walking too fast. I can't keep up with you.

- Jack has cut down on smoking. He only smokes five cigarettes a day

now.
