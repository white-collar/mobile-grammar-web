Unit 54 - Verb + infinitive

Unit 54

Verb + infinitive

(a)

agree offer decide appear forget

refuse attempt plan seem learn (how)

promise manage arrange pretend dare

threaten fail hope afford tend

 

Якщо за цими дієсловами йде ще одне, то

зазвичай використовується конструкція

дієслово + to + інфінітив:

 

- As it was late, we decided to take a taxi home.

- I like George but I think he tends to talk too much.

- How old were you when you learnt to drive? ( або `learnt how to drive')

- They agreed to lend me some money when I told them the position I was in.

 

Зверніть увагу на приклади із запереченням

not to ...:

 

- We decided not to go out because of the weather.

- He pretended not to see me as he passed me in the street.

 

З іншими важливими дієсловами (наприклад, think і

suggest) не можна використовувати форму інфінітива:

 

- Are you thinking of buying a car? ( а не `thinking to buy')

- Tom suggested going to the cinema. (а не `suggested to go')

 

(b) Форми continuous infinitive (to be doing) і

perfect infinitive (to have done) зазвичай

використовуються після seem, appear і pretend:

 

- I pretended to be reading. (= I pretended that I was reading)

- You seem to have lost weight. (= it seems that you have lost weight)

 

(c) Після dare можна використовувати інфінітив

як з to, так і без:

 

 

- I wouldn't dare to ask him. або I wouldn't dare ask him.

 

Але після daren't ви повинні вжити

інфінітив без to:

 

- I daren't tell him what happened. ( а не `daren't to tell')

 

(d) Після дієслів: ask decide know remember

forget explain understand можна ставити

питальне слово (what/where/how тощо)

+ to + інфінітив:

 

We asked how to get to the station.

Have you decided where to go for your holidays?

Tom explained (to me) how to change the wheel of the car.

I don't know whether to go to the party or not.

 

Також:

show/tell/ask someone (комусь) what/how/where

to do something (робити щось):

 

- Can someone show me how to change the film in this camera?

- Ask Jack. He'll tell you what to do.
