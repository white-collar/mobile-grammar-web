Unit 59

Preposition + -ing

(a) Якщо дієслово йде після прийменника

in at with about тощо,

то воно закінчується на -ing.

Розгляньмо приклади:

 

Are you interested

in

working

for us?

I'm fed up

at

learning

languages.

I'm not very good

with

studying.

 

The children are excited

about

going

on holiday.

What are the advantages

of

having

a car?

This knife is only

for

cutting

bread.

John went to work

in spite of

feeling

ill. (Див. Unit 109.)

I bought a new bicycle

instead of

going

away on holiday.

 

(b) Можна вживати -ing з before і after:

 

- Before going out I phoned Ann.

You can also say: `Before I went out I...'

- What did you do after leaving school?

You can also say: `... after you left school?'

 

(c) Ви можете вжити by -ing, щоб сказати,

як щось сталося:

 

- They got into the house by breaking a kitchen window and climbing in.

- You can improve your English by doing a lot of reading.

 

(d) Після without теж можна використовувати -ing:

 

- Tom left without finishing his dinner.

- She ran five miles without stopping.

- He climbed through the window without anybody seeing him.

(або `... without being seen.')

- She needs to work without people disturbing her. (або `... without

being disturbed.')

- It's nice to go on holiday without having to worry about money.

 

(e) To — зазвичай частина інфінітива

(infinitive).

Наприклад:

 

- They decided to go out.

- I want to play tennis.

 

Але to також є й прийменником.

Приклади:

 

- Tom went to London.

- He gave the book to Ann.

- I prefer cities to the countryside.

- I'm looking forward to the week-end.

 

Якщо за прийменником іде дієслово, воно закінчується

на -ing (див. розділ a). Тому якщо to — прийменник

і за ним іде дієслово, треба казати to -ing.

Наприклад:

 

- I prefer cycling to driving (а не `to drive')

- I'm looking forward to seeing Ann again. (а не `to see')
