Unit 119

To, been to, into By car / in my car

 

(a) To

Ми кажемо go to/come to/travel to(тощо), коли

йдеться про місця або події. Наприклад:

 

go to America

come to England

return to Italy

fly to Moscow

walk to work

drive to the airport

go to the bank

go to a party

go to a concert

be sent to prison

be taken to hospital

go to bed

 

Ми кажемо get to (однак arrive in/at -

див. Unit 118e):

 

- What time did you get to London/work/the party?

 

Ми кажемо go home/come home/get home тощо

(без прийменника):

 

- I'm tired. Let's go home. - What time did you get home last night?

 

(b) Been to

I have been to (a place) = Я побував десь; я їздив

туди, але тепер я повернувся (див.Unit 13c):

 

- Have you ever been to Japan?

- I've been to Rome four times.

- Ann has never been to a football match in her life.

- Jack has got plenty of money. He has just been to the bank.

 

(c) Into

Go into/come into тощо = enter

(до кімнати/до будівлі тощо):

 

- I opened the door and went into the room.

- Don't wait outside! Come into the house.

- The man the police were chasing ran into a shop.

- A bird flew into the room through the window.

 

(d) By car / in my car

Ми використовуємо by ..., щоб сказати, яким

чином ми подорожуємо:

 

by car

by train

by plane

by boat/ship

by bus

by bicycle

також:

by road

by rail

by air

by sea

by Underground

 

- `How did you go to Paris?' `By plane.'

- Tom usually goes to work by bicycle / by car / by bus / by train.

 

Однак ми кажемо `on foot':

 

- Did you come here by car or on foot?

 

Однак не можна сказати by, якщо ви кажете

`my car/the train/a taxi' тощо

Кажуть `in my car' (але не `by my car'),

`on the train' (але не `by the train').

 

 

Для машин і таксі ми вживаємо in:

 

in my car in Tom's car in the car in a car in a taxi

Ми кажемо

get in(to)/get out of машини або таксі:

He got into the car and drove off. (або 'He got in the car...')

Для велосипедів і громадського транспорту (автобус, потяг тощо) ми вживаємо on:

 

on my bicycle on the bus on the 6.45 train on a big ship

Ми кажемо get on/get off

(велосипед, автобус або потяг):

 

Quick! Get on the train. It's ready to leave.
