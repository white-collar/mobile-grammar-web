Unit 89 - Relative clauses (2) cluses with or without who/that

Unit 89

Relative clauses (2) cluses with or without who/that

(a) Погляньте ще раз на приклади з Unit 88:

 

- The man who lived next door was a sailor. (або `that lived')

- Where are the eggs that were in the fridge? (або `which were')

 

У цих реченнях who і that є

ПІДМЕТАМИ, присудки яких перебувають в

уточнювальній частині речення: чоловік живе

по сусідству, яйця були в холодильнику. У цих

реченнях who або that не можна пропускати.

 

Іноді who і that є ДОДАТКАМИ при

дієсловах в уточнювальній частині речення:

 

the man - I wanted to see him - was away on holiday

- The man who (або that) I wanted to see was away on holiday.

have you found the keys? - you lost them

- Have you found the keys that you lost?

 

Коли who або that є дієслівними

додатками в уточнювальній частині речення,

тоді ви можете їх пропускати:

 

- The man I wanted to see was away on holiday.(але не `The man I wanted

to see him was away on holiday.')

- Have you found the keys you lost? (але не `Have you found the keys

you lost them?')

- The work Fred did was not difficult. (= the work that Fred did)

- The man Jess is going to marry is Italian. (= the man who/that

Jess is going to marry)

- Is there anything I can do? (= is there anything that I can do?)

 

(b) У відносних підрядних реченнях

часто вживаються прийменники (in/at/with тощо).

Розгляньте місце цих прийменників

у таких реченнях:

 

do you know the girl? - Tom is talking to her

- Do you know the girl (who/that) Tom is talking to ?

the bed - I slept in it last night - wasn't very comfortable

- The bed (that) I slept in last night wasn't very comfortable.

 

- The man (who/that) I sat next to on the plane was very strange.

- Are these the letters (that) you couldn't find?

- The girl (who/that) he fell in love with lives in Pensacola.

 

(c) What не вживається замість that:

 

- Everything (that) he promised was done. (а не `everything what

he promised')

- I gave her all the love (that) I had. (а не `all ... what I had')

 

What = the thing(s) that:

 

- Will he do what I said? (= the words that I said)

- No-one will learn what has happened. (= the thing that has happened)
