Unit 109 - Although / though / even though In spite of / despite

Unit 109

Although / though / even though In spite of / despite

 

(a) Розгляньмо ситуацію:

 

Торік Джек і Джилл провели канікули біля моря.

Було дощово, але вони залишилися задоволені.

 

Ми можемо сказати:

Although it rained a lot, they enjoyed themselves.

(= It rained a lot but they ...)

або: In spite of the rain, they enjoyed themselves.

Despite of the rain, they enjoyed themselves.

 

 

(b) Після although ми вживаємо конструкцію

підмет + присудок:

 

- Although she smokes 40 cigarettes a day, she is quite fit.

- Although it rained a lot, we enjoyed our holiday.

- I didn't get the job although I had all the necessary qualifications.

 

Після in spite of (або despite) ми вживаємо

іменник, займенник (this/that/what

тощо) або -ing:

 

- In spite of the rain, we enjoyed our holiday.

- I didn't get the job despite my qualifications.

- She wasn't well, but in spite of this she went to work.

- Despite what I said last night, I still love you.

- I'm not tired in spite of working hard all day.

 

Зверніть увагу, кажуть `in spite of', але despite

(без of).

Можна також сказати in spite of / despite the

fact (that) ...:

 

- In spite of the fact (that) I was tired, I couldn't sleep.

- She's quite fit despite the fact (that) she smokes 40 cigarettes a day.

 

Порівняйте although і in spite of/despite:

 

- Although the traffic was bad, I arrived on time.

In spite of the traffic, I arrived on time.

- I couldn't sleep although I was very tired.

I couldn't sleep despite being very tired.

 

(c) Іноді ми вживаємо though замість although:

 

- I didn't get the job though I had all the necessary qualifications.

 

У розмовній англійській though часто стоїть

наприкінці речення:

 

- The house isn't very nice. I like the garden though. (= but I like the

garden)

- I see him every day. I've never spoken to him though. (= but I've never

spoken to him)

 

Even though — це сильніша форма слова

although:

 

- Even though I was really tired, I couldn't sleep.
