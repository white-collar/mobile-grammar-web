Unit 98 - So and such

Unit 98

So and such

 

(a) Розгляньте приклади:

 

- I didn't enjoy the book. The story was so stupid.

- I didn't enjoy the book. It was such a stupid story.

 

Ми вживаємо so з прикметником

без іменника: so interesting

 

Ми вживаємо such з прикметником

з іменником: such an interesting story

 

Ми вживаємо so з прислівником: so carefully

 

 

(b) So і such підсилюють значення прикметника.

Ми кажемо:

so long але such a long time

so far але such a long way

so many але such a lot (of)

so much але such a lot (of)

Порівняйте so і such у таких реченнях:

 

- I like Tom and Ann. They are so nice.

- I like Tom and Ann. They are such nice people. (але не `so nice people')

 

Часто ми кажемо so...that..і such...that..:

 

- I was so tired that I went to bed at seven o'clock.

- She worked so hard that she made herself ill.

- It was such lovely weather that we spent the whole day in the garden.

- The book was so good that I couldn't put it down.

It was such a good book that I couldn't put it down.

 

Ви можете пропустити that у цих реченнях:

 

- I was so tired (that) I went to bed at 7 o'clock.

 

(c) У цих реченнях ми вживаємо so і such

по-різному:

 

- I expected the weather to be mush cooler. I didn't expect it to be so

warm. (= as warm as it is)

- I'm tired because I got up at 6 o'clock. I don't usually get up so

early. (= as early as 6 o'clock)

- Hurry up! Don't walk so slowly. (= as slowly as you are walking)

- I was surprised when Jack told me the house was built 100 years

ago. I didn't realise it was so old. (= as old as it is)

I didn't realise it was such an old house.

 

(d) Ми кажемо: so long але `such a long time';

so far але `such a long way'; so many, so much

але `such a lot (of)':

 

- I haven't seen him for so long that I've forgotten what he looks like.

(або ... for such a long time ...)

- I didn't know you lived so far from the city centre. (або ... such a

long way from ...)

- Why did you buy so much food? (або ... such a lot of food?)
