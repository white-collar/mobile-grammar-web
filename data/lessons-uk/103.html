Unit 103

Comparison (3) - as ... as / than

 

(a) Розгляньмо ситуацію:

 

Джо, Генрі й Артур — мільйонери. Вони дуже багаті. У Джо $5 мільйонів, у Генрі — $4 мільйони, а в Артура — $2 мільйони. Отже:

Henry is rich.

 

He is richer than Arthur.

But he isn't as rich as Joe. (= Joe is richer than he is)

 

Ось ще приклади з конструкцією not as ... as:

 

- Jack isn't as old as he looks. (= he looks older than he is.)

- The city centre wasn't as crowded this morning as it usually is. (= it

is usually more crowded)

- Jim didn't do as well in his examination as he had hoped. (= he had

hoped to do better)

- `The weather's better today, isn't it?' `Yes, it's not as cold.'

(= yesterday was colder)

- I don't know as many people as you do. (= you know more people)

 

Ви можете також сказати `not so ... as' (замість

`not as ... as'):

 

- Henry isn't so rich as Joe.

 

(b) Ви також можете вживати as ... as (але не

`so ... as') у реченнях зі стверджувальним

відтінком або в запитаннях:

 

- I'm sorry I'm late. I got here as fast as I could.

- There's plenty of food, so eat as much as you like.

- Let's walk. It's just as quick as taking the bus.

- Can you send me the money as soon as possible, please?

 

Ми також кажемо twice as ... as, three times

as ... as тощо

 

- Petrol is twice as expensive as it was a few years ago.

- Their house is about three times as big as ours.

 

(c) Ми кажемо the same as (але не `the same like'):

 

- Ann's salary is the same as mine. (або Ann gets the same salary as me.)

- Tom is the same age as George.

- `What would you like to drink?' `I'll have the same as last time.'

 

(d) Після than і as частіше вживаються займенники

me/him/her/them/us, коли в реченні немає

другого дієслова. Порівняйте:

 

- You are taller than I am. але: You are taller than me.

- They have more money than але: They have more money than us.

we have.

- I can't run as fast as he can. але: I can't run as fast as him.
