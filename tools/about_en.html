Mobile Grammar: English 2.0

Why this app?

This app is a mobile reference of articles on English grammar. There are 130 of them, and they cover all the main topics of English grammar. It is based on materials of the Cambridge textbook for foreign students learning on their own. The explanations are in Russian and, on this site, also in Ukrainian.

"Mobile Grammar" has a history

"Mobile Grammar: English 2.0" is already the second version of the app: the first one was, unfortunately, removed from the Play Market for technical reasons, and its original code seems to be lost for good. Back in 2012 it had 160 articles; some of them have since been merged. The app was discussed on the popular forum 4pda.ru and, for example, on AndroidTop. In total "Mobile Grammar" had about 17,000 users and 364 ratings, averaging 4.46.

Considering that I put the first version together literally a couple of hours before a Java job interview, that it didn't even have its own launcher icon and that its design was simply awful, I'd like to thank all users who found the app useful. Thank you!
